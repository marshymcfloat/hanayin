## Context

Describe the problem, current constraints, and why a decision is needed now.
Include verified evidence and links to relevant code, incidents, metrics, plans,
or prior decisions. Do not place secrets or sensitive customer data here.

## Decision Drivers

- State the most important requirement or constraint.
- Include security, tenant isolation, reliability, performance, cost,
  maintainability, and migration concerns when relevant.
- Distinguish mandatory constraints from preferences.

## Decision

State the selected option and the reasons it best satisfies the decision
drivers. Be explicit about boundaries, ownership, and behavior that future work
must preserve.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Expected improvement.

### Negative

- Accepted cost or limitation.

### Neutral or Follow-up

- Required migration, documentation, monitoring, or later decision.

## Implementation and Rollout

- Describe the safe migration sequence.
- State compatibility or rollback requirements.
- Identify required documentation and ownership changes.

## Validation

- Define tests, metrics, security checks, or operational signals that prove the

- Define conditions that should trigger review or reversal.
